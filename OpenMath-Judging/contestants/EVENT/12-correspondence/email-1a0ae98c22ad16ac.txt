Dear Alejandro,

Thank you for reaching out and letting me know about your event. Unfortunately, I personally feel strongly against the conception that mathematics is a competitive activity. The current increase in the pace of production is quite concerning to me, and I believe events which focus on creating a large amount of output in a short amount of time are detrimental to the community as a whole. I appreciate that your event welcomes non-AI contributions and good quality formalisations of known concepts, but I would much rather offer my support to events which offer the opportunity to think deeply about what the future of mathematics should be, or which offer training in valuable skills which will contribute positively to our field. For these reasons, I will not be advertising this event, but I would be happy to hear from you if you organise further events more in line with my values.

Best regards,
