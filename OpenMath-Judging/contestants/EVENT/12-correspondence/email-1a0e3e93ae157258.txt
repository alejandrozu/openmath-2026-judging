Hi Yiming,

I am helping organize OpenMath at MIT, and I thought this could
interest people from your interactive theorem-proving course at LMU.
The aim is to produce as much useful, formally verified mathematics as
we can in one week.

Would you be willing to share the invitation with interested students
or colleagues? They could form a small team around a manageable new
formalization, rather than feeling they need to tackle a famous
conjecture. Known-mathematics formalization has a separate
leaderboard, and human-only work is welcome alongside AI-assisted
work. This could be a practical opportunity to turn course experience
into a public, credited mathematical contribution.

The hybrid kickoff is at MIT CSAIL on Sunday, September 27, 2026, at
noon Eastern / 9 a.m. Pacific, with work continuing through Friday,
October 2. You can participate entirely online; attending the opening
is not required. Any AI tools, or human-only work, are welcome. Only
formally checked and mathematically reviewed results count.

Details, handbook and registration: https://rsihouse.ai/openmath
