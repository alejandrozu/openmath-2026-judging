# Structure of the support-138 scheme (analysis of 2026-09-30)

Object: `solutions/support_138/solution.json` (rank 23, support 138, exact over Q, coefficients ±2^k with k in −4..4). It is compared with `solutions/official_139/` (Smirnov's class, support 139) and with Laderman 1976, which was rebuilt from the literature in `common.py` and has support 153. All three pass `brent.brent_errors == 0`. All scripts and outputs are in `~/Projects/mm3-kaggle/analysis/`. The research repo was not modified.

Convention: factors are 3x3 matrices stored row-major, for A (i,j), B (j,k) and C (k,i), with the tensor tr(ABC), as in `src/brent.py`. The symmetries used are:

- the sandwich A → PAQ⁻¹, B → QBR⁻¹, C → RCP⁻¹, which acts on a factor u as Pᵀ u Q⁻ᵀ;
- the cyclic shift of the three slots;
- the transpose (u, v, w) → (wᵀ, vᵀ, uᵀ).

Each was checked exactly to preserve validity. "Gauge" means term rescaling (u·a, v·b, w/(ab)) combined with a diagonal sandwich.

## Headline

1. **Exact symmetry.** The 138 has an exact symmetry of order 2: it fixes 3 terms and swaps 10 pairs. The 139 has a symmetry of the same shape on its zero pattern only; no choice of signs or diagonal scalings makes it exact.
2. **Few constants after rescaling.** Up to gauge, the 138 needs only 12 non-±1 coefficients, all ±2 or ±1/2. This is an exact MILP optimum, and it stays 12 even when real (non-dyadic) diagonal scalings are allowed. The stored form has 73. The rescaled form is in `support_138_gauged/solution.json`, verified independently: 0 failing identities, support 138, 12 non-±1 entries.
3. **The family is essentially a curve.** The 7-dimensional tangent family consists of 6 diagonal-gauge directions and 1 genuine direction. The genuine direction is an explicit rational curve in a parameter s, with the 138 at s = −1. All three ends of the curve (s → 0, −2, ∞) are degenerate. No support-137 scheme lies on the family or at its boundary.
4. **No entry or term can be removed** (experiment B). The best residual after forcing an entry to zero was 1.2e-5. It approaches zero only while coefficients grow like residual^(−1/6), which is a border-rank-type approach, not a real scheme.
5. **Not a small edit of the 139.** After aligning the two schemes on their common core, 4 of the 23 terms are identical and 19 differ.
6. **Practical cost.** The 138 needs 83 additions, against 84 for the 139 and 98 for Laderman. Its 12 constants are all powers of 2, so they are bit shifts. In float64 the rescaled form is as accurate as the 139 and Laderman.

## B. Forcing one entry or one term to zero

Scripts: `local_opt.py` and `run_local_opt.sh` (outputs `local_opt_entries.jsonl`, `local_opt_terms.jsonl`, `local_opt_table.md`), plus `followup.py` and `probe_limit.py`.

**Setup.**
- For each of the 138 entries, that entry was set to 0, the other 137 were left free, and everything outside the pattern was held at 0.
- Each run used 40 starts:
  - 8 near the family: the 138 point and 7 exact family points, with the entry zeroed; half of them with 5% multiplicative noise;

- Solver: Levenberg-Marquardt on the normal equations, 400 iterations per start (`lsq.solve_fast`). Each entry took about 100–230 s on 4 workers.

## C. Practical metrics

Script: `metrics.py` (output in `metrics_out.txt`).

Definitions:
- **Additions:** the sum over terms of (nnz(u)−1)+(nnz(v)−1), plus the sum over the 9 outputs of (number of contributing terms − 1). For every scheme here this equals support − 55.
- **Growth factor e:** the maximum over outputs of Σ_k |w_k[c]|·‖u_k‖₁·‖v_k‖₁. Term rescaling leaves it unchanged; diagonal gauge changes it. The "minimum over diagonal gauge" row uses Nelder-Mead with 20 starts.
- **Relative error:** 10,000 trials with A and B uniform in [−1,1] (seed 7), evaluated in float64 and compared with the exact product in Fractions. The measure is max_ik |ΔC| / (max|A|·max|B|).

## F. The exotic 139s are the 138 in disguise (2026-10-01)

Full report: `lift139/REPORT_lift139.md`. Both exotic 139 classes lift to exact rational schemes (0 failing identities, support 139, 16 non-±1 coefficients, denominators 1 and 2 only; re-verified independently). They are one scheme up to symmetry: the 138's order-2 symmetry maps class A onto class B. In the 138's own frame, class A shares 21 of 23 terms with the 138 exactly; the other two terms on each side share the factor v = E_{3,2}, sum to the same rank-2 tensor, and differ only by a GL(2) change of basis on that pair. Smirnov's 139 is unrelated (44 pattern entries away). The 138 stays the unique known scheme below 139, and the only known mod-2-invisible family at this level.

## L. Core 1809: a new, mod-2-invisible rank-23 scheme at support 143 (2026-10-02)

Full report: `core1809/REPORT_core1809.md` (+ `.docx`). The two census hits on core 1809 are one scheme: the second is the image of the first under one of the six symmetries of core 1809 (Smirnov's core has two). The exact lift succeeded (8 of 64 starts, residual 1.2e-15, every parameter snapped to the dyadic grid, exact repair): **0 failing identities, support 143, 35 non-±1 coefficients, denominators 1 and 2 only** (re-verified independently for both frames, `core1809/lift_143_1/solution.json`, `lift_143_2/`). Exact family dimension 1, like the 138.

Why it is new:
- Its mod-2 pattern matches none of the 137 known scheme files, including the 14 published schemes at support 143, under any of the 1,296 maps.
- It is not a rewrite of anything on Smirnov's core: the best alignment shares only 4 of 23 terms with the 138 (pattern difference 43) and 2 with Smirnov's 139 (68); the nearest published scheme shares 4 terms (difference 42). The exact term-level test confirms 4 of 23 terms equal as tensors versus the 138, all of them single-product terms.
- It lives on a different core: a different pairing of the 27 needed products into shared terms, inequivalent to Smirnov's under all symmetries.

Structure: the same skeleton as the 138 and the 139 (4 pair-terms carrying the planted pairs, 19 single-product terms, every needed product made by exactly one term), with 78 distinct unwanted products (162 incidences, 72 of multiplicity 2 and 6 of multiplicity 3; the 138 has 69/142, the 139 has 68/136). The pattern has a trivial automorphism group.

Local optimality: forcing any one of its 143 nonzeros to zero and re-solving (40 starts each) never reaches residual below 8.5e-7; the 81 needed-product entries give residual 1, the 62 cancellation entries give 8.5e-7 to 1.6e-3, and the two smallest are border-type limits (the residual stalls while coefficients drift to infinity, the same signature as the 138's near-misses). Tracing its 1-parameter family in both directions, no entry vanishes at finite distance. So it is locally optimal, like the 138.

Reading: the first scheme found by this method that is neither Smirnov's family nor the 138's. It is invisible mod 2 (not a valid GF(2) pattern), so no GF(2) search could have found it. At 143 it is not sparse by the leaderboard's standard, but it shows the mod-3 landscape holds families on other cores. Wave 7 walks core 1809 at caps 140-142 to see whether this family goes lower.
