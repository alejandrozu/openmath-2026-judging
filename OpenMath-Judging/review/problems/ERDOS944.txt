For a finite simple graph G with n vertices and an integer r >= 1, suppose its chromatic number is 4, deleting any vertex lowers that number, and deleting any set of at most r edges does not lower it. Then 27r+19 <= 4n. The same hypotheses give 27(r+1)(n-1) <= (2n+1)^2 and 9b(r+1) <= (2b+1)(n-b), where b=floor((n+1)/3). These necessary conditions form one family.
