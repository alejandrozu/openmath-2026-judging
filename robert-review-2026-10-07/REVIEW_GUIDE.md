# Review guide

Start with the [11-page concise Robert manuscript](manuscript/Robert_Huynh_concise.pdf). It states the eight families, their short mathematical proofs, exact hypotheses and known overlap. Its proof references are pinned to an immutable source snapshot rather than an evolving branch.

Then read [Robert's eight-family novelty/proof review](research/Robert8_current_novelty_and_proof_scope.md) and the [other nineteen-family priority followup](research/original19_priority_followup_20261007.md). Each identifies the exact proposed delta, a primary predecessor, what is still unknown and the best concrete improvement to pursue. The deeper [five-family matrix](research/research_five_comparison_matrix.md) retains theorem-level source locators and retrieval limits.

For proof review, use the [exact selected-declaration index](proofs/selected-endpoints.json), not a raw count of source files. Every additional selected declaration links a source hash and an actual successful compiler receipt. [Written dossiers](written-proofs/manifest.json) preserve full ordinary arguments. [The cherry correction](written-proofs/BM_cherry_correction.md) explains a repaired missing hypothesis.

The main BM Hamiltonicity proof, avoidance-conditioned E110 bridge, private unavailable proofs, finite11/12 upper exclusions and eighteen-vertex minimality are not promoted to completed formal theorems. Known literature and third-party proof foundations receive attribution, not new-family credit. The unchanged old nineteen standings and the later proposed twenty-seven-family judging annex remain separate dated decisions.

Judged and evaluated by Alejandro Zarzuelo Urdiales in connection with OpenMath2026 at Harvard and MIT. This archive is prepared for author and human mathematical review; it is not a journal acceptance or an institutional endorsement.
