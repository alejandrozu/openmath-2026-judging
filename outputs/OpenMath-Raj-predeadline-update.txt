Raj Harshit: Here's where we are, briefly:   - New upper bound: K(18) ≤ 94 for all arrangements of 18 lines or pseudolines, with points of any multiplicity. As far as we know, the best previous bounds allowing multiple points were Tamura's 96 and the unpublished 95 of Clément–Bader. The 93 bound of Blanc (and the 94 of Bartholdi–Blanc–Loisel) covers simple arrangements only. - K(18) = 93 when no four lines meet, i.e. Blanc's simple-arrangement result extended to triple points. - Structure of any 94: it can only contain triple points and two kinds of fourfold point, and it must contain an "all-8" fourfold point (all 8 angles triangles). That one case is the only thing left between us and K(18) = 93. We've reduced it to a single inequality in nonnegative local credits, which is still open. - Construction: a new 93-triangle arrangement with no multiple points, found by unseeded search and scored 93 by the official evaluator. That ties the record; it is not a new one.   The proofs are computer-assisted: exact rational LP certificates checked by dynamic programming over an automaton of all possible lines, plus SAT exclusions with DRAT proofs.They haven't been formalised, and there are no Lean files; we'll label that clearly.   The repo, with the paper (PDF and LaTeX), certificates, an independent checker and reproduction commands, will be up by tonight.
