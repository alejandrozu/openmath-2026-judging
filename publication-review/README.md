## Publication decision and verification limits

On 5 October 2026 the chair authorized uploading the current papers and judging material to this public archive and waived the remaining proof runs for this upload. This is a publication decision, not a completed-proof or novelty verdict. Actual successful checks, unrun requests, failures, admitted assumptions and native-computation trust remain recorded separately.

- **HTPeo Ramsey:** the qualified partial replay covers 2083 of 2103 source modules. Twenty sources and the original and supplemental final audit routes remain unrun; the selected 24-endpoint scope is not qualified as complete. Earlier successful rows and both resource-stop attempts remain preserved.
- **A000224:** 167 of 171 sources are qualified, including Bounds. Four sources and the three selected endpoint prints remain unrun. The claim concerns three ordered distinct odd primes with the zero-inclusive square-residue count; it is not the unrestricted A000224 conjecture.
- **DMS:** a separately qualified body-identical composite covers all 228 scientific bodies through 99 original-owned outputs, 122 V3-owned passes and seven V4 checks. A one-import supplement audits the same 123 requests: 120 standard-kernel and three axiom-free. The original 119-source checkpoint and failed 120/123 audit are preserved. An imported finite certificate outside the selected set uses native_decide. Five structural premises and the general DMS conjecture remain open; chair-retained **p=.15** is unchanged.
- **Luke/Madhan and Matt:** the papers retain their exact selected theorem scopes, prior-work and originality caveats. Luke's finite native-evaluation trust is explicit. Matt's selected generic pivot theorem is separate from admitted baselines and the unproved universal 21/48 circuit claim. Their proposed scores and resource classes remain conditional.
- **Leanification:** mention only. Known-mathematics formalization is recorded separately from original-result scoring.

Earlier October 3/4 archive documents and tables are preserved history. The dated amended proposals and source-bound records in this review set govern its stated scope. Authorship and affiliation qualifications, original licenses and contributor credits are retained. Publishing this archive does not send the preserved communication drafts or imply institutional endorsement.
